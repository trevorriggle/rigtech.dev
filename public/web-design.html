The number nobody could produce

American Scientific is a wholesaler. Almost nobody pays list: what an account pays depends on its price level and the quantity on the line, and NetSuite, the ERP the business runs on, holds all of it exactly.

The old site synced a base price and threw the rest away, then a WordPress plugin rebuilt each customer's tier from their user role. The price a customer saw was a guess at a number that was one API call away.

What was actually running

Thirty-plus plugins, all depending on each other, none of them upgraded. The catalog ran off a NetSuite saved search called DO NOT DELETE that nobody still at the company could explain.

By the end most product images no longer loaded and the sync had quietly failed. Nothing alerted.

What replaced it

A Next.js app on Cloudflare Workers with D1 as the catalog store, and a second Worker that owns the NetSuite integration. Nothing that touches the ERP runs in the same process as a page.

The site asks NetSuite for the account's price level and shows the real figure for the quantity. The catalog is one SQL query, in version control.
