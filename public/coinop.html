A roguelite coin pusher where real coins have powers.

Push the rare bank note into the tray before your pouch runs out. Win the stage, spend your winnings, and take on a stranger machine. Lose the run and the note stays on the shelf, smug.

What makes it different

  1. 80+

    Special coins, based on real ones

    A Buffalo nickel charges straight down its lane. A Standing Liberty quarter walls off the nearest gap. A Bully shoves six coins far too hard. Every coin does something.

  2. 4→1

    Fuse them in the Mint

    Roll a Mule Die and fuse up to four coins into one Mule that does all of their jobs in a single drop. Thousands of combinations; some of them are a terrible idea.

  3. 19

    Stages, each with its own twist

    A laundromat, a sand trap, pop bumpers, a river, a paperclipped note, and a vault where the note is torn in half.

  4. 4

    Bosses, and then the machine

    Four boss stages bend the rules of the board. The final boss isn't on the machine. It is the machine.

  5. 78

    A collector's Album

    Every coin, Mule and prize you find gets a slot. 78 pieces. You'll know which one you're missing.

  6. 1/day

    Daily Machines

    A new machine every day, the same one for everyone. Retry until you beat it and keep your streak alive.
