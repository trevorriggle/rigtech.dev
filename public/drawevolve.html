DrawEvolve

Draw first. Then get coached.

A drawing app where the AI coaches instead of critiques. It tells you what's working, gives you one thing to fix, and remembers what it told you last time.

drawevolve.com ↗

How it works

  1. 01/05

    The critique itself: a quick take, what's working, then one focus area, in that order.

  2. 02/05

    Eve picks the critique up in conversation and answers with a pass to run on a new layer.

  3. 03/05

    Ask for the one thing worth fixing and you get one thing. Five priorities is none.

  4. 04/05

    An on-device model guesses where the eye lands. You mark where you meant it to.

  5. 05/05

    The gap between where the eye goes and where it was sent is the whole critique.
