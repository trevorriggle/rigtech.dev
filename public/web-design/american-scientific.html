The brief

American Scientific sells lab and classroom supplies wholesale, mostly to schools. Nearly every customer has their own pricing, and the website needed to show it.

It also needed to look like a company that has been doing this for decades, because it has.

What changed

A new site, designed from scratch. Clear categories with a real product count on each, a home page that invites schools to open an account, and room for the catalog to grow.

Prices come straight from the company's own records, so a signed-in customer sees what they'll actually pay.

The switch

Changing sites shouldn't cost a business its customers. Every account came across, old links and bookmarks still land on the right page, and carts left in the old store were waiting in the new one.
