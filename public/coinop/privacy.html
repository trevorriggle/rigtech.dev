Coin-Op Privacy Policy

RIG Tech LLC · Effective November 10, 2026

Coin-Op is a coin pusher game for iPhone made by RIG Tech LLC ("we", "us"), based in Columbus, Ohio, USA. This policy explains what information the app handles, who handles it, and the choices you have.

Short version: we don't ask for or collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and there are no accounts. Your game progress lives on your phone and in your own iCloud. The app uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics, which tells us how the game is used (in aggregate, without identifying you by name), and AdMob, which shows ads. Both collect some device data, described below. You can limit this by declining tracking when iOS asks.

What Coin-Op stores, and where

  • Your game progress (levels, coins, album, stats, settings) is saved on your device.
  • iCloud sync: if you're signed in to iCloud, the same progress is copied to your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it follows you to a new device. Apple stores it under your Apple ID. We can't see it.
  • We don't run our own servers for Coin-Op, and the game doesn't send your progress, name, email, contacts, photos or location to us.

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how people play Coin-Op, so we can fix problems and make the game better (for example: which machines players get stuck on, how long sessions last, whether an update caused crashes or drop-off). Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • a random app-instance identifier that Firebase creates when the app is installed (it resets if you delete and reinstall the app);
  • gameplay and usage events, such as when the app is opened, levels started and finished, screens viewed, ads shown, and in-app purchases made (we see that a purchase happened, never your payment details);
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, language, app version, and session length and frequency;
  • approximate location (country, region or city) derived from your IP address. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses;
  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

We see this information in aggregate reports in the Firebase console. We don't use it to identify you personally, and we don't sell it. Google processes it on our behalf. See Privacy and Security in Firebase, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Coin-Op is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob: occasional ads between rounds, and optional "watch an ad" rewards that you choose to play. To serve and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • general device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version), approximate location based on IP address, and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information related to ads.

Google uses this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure ad performance. If you allow tracking (and, where required, give consent), ads may be personalized based on your interests; otherwise you'll see non-personalized ads, which are based on general context such as the app and your approximate location. See How Google uses advertising data, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices and how to opt out

  • Tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks whether Coin-Op may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", or later turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, neither AdMob nor Firebase Analytics can access your advertising identifier, and ads are not personalized using it.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK, and certain US states), Coin-Op shows Google's consent message so you can accept, reject or choose which kinds of ad and analytics data processing you allow. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS (shown where the law requires it).
  • Google ad settings: you can control ad personalization across Google services at My Ad Center.
  • No Ads: an optional in-app purchase removes the ads between rounds. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Stopping all collection: deleting the app stops all data collection by Coin-Op and the Google services inside it.
  • Deleting your data: deleting the app removes the data on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone. Because we don't know who you are, we can't look up analytics data by name or email. If you'd like us to request deletion of analytics data, email us and we'll do what we reasonably can.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers described above (Apple for iCloud and purchases, Google for analytics and ads), or if required by law. Under some US state laws, letting an ad network use device identifiers to show personalized ads may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising. You can opt out of that by declining tracking, or through the in-game privacy options where shown.

Children

Coin-Op is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Coin-Op are limited to general-audience content. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us. Where EU/UK law applies, we rely on your consent for personalized ads and analytics, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
