Descriptions

Short

CoinOp is a roguelite coin pusher for iPhone and iPad where real coins have powers. Push the rare bank note off the shelf before your pouch runs out, fuse coins into Mules, and beat 19 stages and the machine itself. Launching December 16.

Long

Push the rare bank note into the tray before your pouch runs out.

CoinOp is a coin pusher with a roguelite run. Drop coins, line up your shots, and shove the note off the shelf. Win the stage, spend your cash at the Coin Shop, and take on the next, stranger stage. Run out of coins with the note still on the shelf and the run is over.

There are more than 80 special coins, many based on real ones. A Buffalo nickel charges straight down its lane; a Standing Liberty quarter walls off the nearest gap. In the Mint, a roll of the Mule Die fuses up to four coins into one Mule that does all of their jobs in a single drop.

Nineteen stages each have their own twist: open sides, sand traps, a river, pop bumpers, a paperclipped note, and a vault where the note is torn in half. Boss stages bend the rules, and the final boss is the machine itself. Every coin, Mule and prize you find goes into a 78-piece collector's Album, and a new Daily Machine, the same for everyone, arrives every day.

No account. No energy timers. Just you and the machine.
